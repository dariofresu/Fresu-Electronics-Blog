You don't have to be 10 years into PCB Design to become an EMI specialist.

2024-10-04 · Original on Wix

Categories: EMI - Short Post · Tags: EMI, EMC, Hardware Design

In fact, I bet you already have a lot of knowledge on the topic, but what I see people struggling with the most is putting things together.

I know because I was in the same place too.

My approach was to first design the schematics, and then convert it into PCB Layout.

Pretty straightforward, right?

More or less...

Until the board would suddenly freeze, or some LED would randomly switch on and off, or some components would start to heat up randomly.

I mean, it was that bad.

Little did I know that the problem was actually in how my layout was designed.

Not because the connections from point to point weren’t correct or robust, but because the project wasn’t designed with electromagnetic fields in mind.

The good part is that I didn’t have to go through complex equations to make a U-turn and set the project right from the start.

In fact, I dare to say that the concepts are pretty simple to grasp.

And if you want to know what these concepts are, I’m sharing them here for free:
